'@novastarter/database-driver-mysql': minor
---

Add `DatabaseDriverMysql`, a mysql2 driver for `@novastarter/database`: a `mysql2/promise` pool from a URI, pool options or a pool of the caller's own, Drizzle's `MySql2Database` with the relational-query `mode` (`default` or `planetscale`) and optional query logging, and `migrate()` over drizzle-kit folders.
