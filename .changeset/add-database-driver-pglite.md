Add `DatabaseDriverPglite`, a PGlite driver for `@novastarter/database`: Postgres in-process on WebAssembly, on `memory://` or a directory created when missing, Drizzle's `PgliteDatabase` sharing `PgDatabase` with the other Postgres drivers, and `migrate()` over drizzle-kit folders.
